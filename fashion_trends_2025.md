# 2025 Fashion Trends — Sources from 2023–2024

## Summary
The fashion industry faces economic uncertainty and consumer behavior shifts in 2025. Key themes across sources: sustainability, AI integration, Y2K revival, quiet luxury, and structural market deceleration. Below are the most relevant reports and articles from 2023–2024 with their predictions for 2025.

---

## 1. McKinsey & Company — The State of Fashion 2025
- **URL**: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/retail/our-insights
- **Date**: November 2024 (report publication); video posted February 2025
- **Key predictions**:
  - Global fashion market to post low single-digit growth in 2025 (structural deceleration post-pandemic boom)
  - Non-luxury segment will drive growth in 2024–2025; luxury led in prior years but slows
  - Brands must prioritize capturing market share amid muted consumer confidence
  - Emphasis on authenticity and emotional connections with brands
  - Leveraging AR and Gen AI as key technological advancements
